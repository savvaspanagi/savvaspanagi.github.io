## Overview

**Project 1:** In collaboration with the TSO and DSO of Cyprus, developed technical requirements for integrating Energy Storage Systems (ESS) into the power grid and technical standards for grid-forming inverters to support voltage and frequency regulation.

## Focus areas

- **ESS grid integration**: technical requirements for connecting Energy Storage Systems to the Cyprus power system
- **Grid-forming inverters**: technical standards to support voltage and frequency regulation
- **TSO–DSO collaboration**: alignment of requirements across transmission and distribution stakeholders

## Context

This work was carried out at the Sustainable Power Systems Laboratory (Cyprus University of Technology), in collaboration with the Transmission System Operator and Distribution System Operator of Cyprus.
