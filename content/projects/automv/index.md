## Overview

**AutoMV** develops an automated framework for converting Geographic Information System (GIS) data into simulation-ready medium-voltage (MV) distribution network models in DIgSILENT PowerFactory.

The work supports faster, more reproducible electrical modeling and analysis of distribution networks, reducing manual model-building effort and improving consistency between geospatial asset data and power-system studies.

## Focus areas

- GIS-to-electrical model conversion pipelines
- MV distribution network topology and asset mapping
- Simulation-ready PowerFactory models for planning and analysis

## Context

AutoMV is carried out at the Sustainable Power Systems Laboratory (Cyprus University of Technology), alongside related research on distribution-system modeling and flexibility.
